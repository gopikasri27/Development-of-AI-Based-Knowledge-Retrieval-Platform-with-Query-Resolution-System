# GlobalTech HR Policies and Employee Handbook

## 1. Annual Leave and Sick Leave Policy
All full-time employees at GlobalTech are entitled to 20 days of paid Annual Leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues on the first day of each month at a rate of 1.66 days. Unused leave up to 5 days can be carried over to the subsequent calendar year, provided it is utilized before March 31st. In addition, employees receive 10 days of Paid Sick Leave annually. For sick leaves exceeding 3 consecutive business days, a medical certificate signed by a licensed physician must be submitted to the HR portal within 48 hours of returning to work.

## 2. Remote and Hybrid Work Guidelines
GlobalTech operates on a 3-2 hybrid model. Employees are required to work from their designated corporate office 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday) and may work remotely up to 2 days per week (Monday and Friday). Fully remote work requests require approval from the Department Head and the Vice President of People Operations. Remote employees must maintain high-speed internet connectivity (minimum 50 Mbps) and adhere to core working hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local timezone.

## 3. Comprehensive Health Insurance and Wellness
GlobalTech provides comprehensive health insurance coverage through BlueShield Enterprise for employees and their eligible dependents. The plan covers 100% of preventive care, 85% of in-network specialist visits, and includes prescription drug coverage with a $15 co-pay for generic medications. An annual wellness allowance of $600 is available for gym memberships, mental health apps, and ergonomic home office accessories.

## 4. Performance Appraisal and Promotion Cycle
Performance reviews are conducted bi-annually in June (Mid-Year Review) and December (Annual Review). Ratings range on a 5-point scale from 1 (Needs Significant Improvement) to 5 (Exceeds Expectations). Promotions and annual compensation adjustments (merit increases) take effect on February 1st following the annual review cycle.
